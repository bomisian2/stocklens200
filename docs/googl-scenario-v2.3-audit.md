# GOOGL v2.3 scenario-based valuation audit

Audit date: 2026-10-12

Financial evidence through: 2026-07-22

Market snapshot: 2026-10-09 close

Research model: `googl-scenario-research-v2.3.0`

Operational status: **hold; no formal fair value and no buy/sell signal**

This report adds usable research scenarios without changing the v2.2 formal FCFF gate. It does not add new issuer financial
periods, analyst consensus, a formal WACC, or an operational valuation. The machine-readable assumptions are in
[`evidence/googl-scenarios-v2.3.json`](evidence/googl-scenarios-v2.3.json), and the deterministic audit is generated by
[`scripts/audit_googl_scenarios.py`](../scripts/audit_googl_scenarios.py).

## 1. Facts, guidance, estimates, and missing data

| State | Meaning | GOOGL v2.3 examples | May become an operational result automatically? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `observed` | Dated value in a traceable source | TTM revenue, operating income, D&A, CAPEX, diluted shares, Treasury rate | No; other gates still apply |
| `issuer_guidance` | Issuer forward range, not an actual result | 2026 CAPEX $175B-$185B | No |
| `model_assumption` | Research stress input with rationale | growth, margin, tax, D&A ratio, working-capital ratio, post-2026 CAPEX, WACC/g grid | No |
| `missing` | Not verified; remains null | revenue/margin guidance, beta, debt yield, market-value debt, marginal operating tax | No |

No new financial actual was acquired in v2.3. The audit reuses the 11 official `FinancialPeriod` records and verified TTM
facts from v2.2. It adds only explicitly labelled research assumptions. The $175B-$185B CAPEX range remains issuer guidance,
and its midpoint is a model assumption rather than a management forecast.

## 2. Formal FCFF versus scenario FCFF

Formal FCFF remains unavailable because a verified operating tax rate and complete operating working-capital change are
missing. Scenario FCFF follows the same economic sequence but supplies future fields as visible assumptions:

`revenue -> operating margin -> EBIT -> assumed operating tax -> NOPAT -> D&A - CAPEX - change in operating NWC -> FCFF`

The scenario engine never writes its result into `formal_fcff`. It retains negative early FCFF instead of adding back CAPEX
or assuming that current AI investment is temporary. Every path uses a non-zero working-capital ratio; missing working capital
is not silently set to zero. One-to-three-year CAPEX/growth timing tags are recorded across scenarios for comparison, but
they are not treated as evidence that investment causes a specific amount of revenue.

Projected FCFF in USD billions:

| Research path | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 | Year 4 | Year 5 | CAPEX/growth lag tag | Meaning |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Downside | -41.251 | -20.769 | 11.397 | 46.478 | 72.302 | 3 years | sustained heavy reinvestment and margin pressure |
| Reference | -20.178 | 10.648 | 53.882 | 94.729 | 134.008 | 2 years | central stress path, **not** an official base case |
| Upside | 0.993 | 44.863 | 99.251 | 151.315 | 194.373 | 1 year | high growth, margin expansion, and improving CAPEX intensity |

All three paths pass the internal positive-growth/reinvestment consistency check. That check is only a contradiction filter;
passing it does not validate the economic forecast.

## 3. WACC evidence and sensitivity

The 10-year U.S. Treasury observation of 5.24% is retained as an observed component. Damodaran's September 2026 implied
ERP of 4.14% is retained as an external research reference, not an official fact or a selected StockLens ERP. A validated
point-in-time Alphabet beta, pre-tax debt cost, debt market value, and marginal operating tax rate remain missing. Formal
WACC is therefore null.

The 9.0%-15.0% rates below are a mechanical sensitivity grid. None is labelled correct, expected, or default. For the
reference research path, enterprise value in USD billions is:

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 9.0% | 1,365.0 ⚠ | 1,455.4 ⚠ | 1,559.7 ⚠ | 1,681.4 ⚠ |
| 10.5% | 1,092.6 ⚠ | 1,151.4 ⚠ | 1,217.5 ⚠ | 1,292.4 ⚠ |
| 12.0% | 900.1 ⚠ | 940.7 ⚠ | 985.5 ⚠ | 1,035.3 ⚠ |
| 13.5% | 757.3 | 786.6 ⚠ | 818.5 ⚠ | 853.5 ⚠ |
| 15.0% | 647.6 | 669.5 | 693.1 | 718.6 |

`⚠` means terminal value exceeds 80% of enterprise value. The engine also flags a WACC-minus-growth spread below 2%p.
Even unflagged cells are research-only. The high terminal dependence means the model is structurally sensitive to distant
cash flows; a visually precise number would overstate confidence.

## 5. CAPEX sensitivity

Holding the reference research assumptions and post-year-one CAPEX path constant, changing only year-one CAPEX within the
official 2026 guide produces these non-operational stable-cell enterprise-value ranges:

| Year-one CAPEX input | State | Stable-cell EV range |
|---|---|---:|
| $175B guide low | issuer guidance boundary | $651.996B-$791.009B |
| $180B midpoint | model assumption | $647.648B-$757.346B |
| $185B guide high | issuer guidance boundary | $643.300B-$752.941B |

This is not a CAPEX normalization. Post-2026 CAPEX ratios, revenue growth, margins, taxes, D&A, and working capital remain
assumptions. The result shows that one-year CAPEX variation is smaller than the uncertainty created by WACC, terminal growth,
and the unverified multi-year operating path.

## 6. Market-implied conditions

At $351.66 and 12.274B diluted shares, the observed common-equity market value diagnostic is $4,316.275B. The model does
not solve a unique required revenue growth, margin, or reinvestment path because:

1. the enterprise-to-equity bridge lacks current debt-like claims, NCI, other claims, and a classification of non-marketable
   securities;
2. one observed equity value cannot uniquely identify seven interacting paths: growth, margin, tax, CAPEX productivity/lag,
   working capital, WACC, and terminal growth;
3. forcing one combination to match price would be calibration to the answer, not independent valuation evidence.

The result therefore remains `underdetermined`, not a forecast or price target. The previous 59.02% reverse FCF CAGR remains
a separate legacy diagnostic based on the CFO-minus-CAPEX proxy and is not promoted to a v2.3 operating expectation.

## 7. Model-to-model differences

- The legacy $64.44 output discounts an issuer-defined CFO-minus-CAPEX equity cash-flow proxy and produces a per-share
  research value. It does not construct FCFF or an enterprise-to-equity bridge.
- The v2.2 formal path requires verified NOPAT, operating working capital, WACC, and a complete equity bridge; it correctly
  returns no value while those inputs are missing.
- The v2.3 scenario path projects assumption-labelled FCFF and returns only enterprise-value sensitivity. It does not divide
  EV by shares, choose a formal base case, or replace the v2.2 gate.

The three results answer different questions and are not averaged. In particular, the scenario EV range must not be used to
argue that the old $64.44 equity-proxy result is either correct or incorrect.

## 8. Independent confidence dimensions

| Dimension | Result |
|---|---|
| Financial data quality | verified official source with unaudited interim periods |
| Forward outlook quality | insufficient except one-year CAPEX range |
| Scenario internal consistency | research checks passed; economic forecasts unvalidated |
| WACC quality | sensitivity only; formal WACC missing |
| Equity bridge quality | incomplete |
| Formal fair value | unavailable |
| Operational investment eligibility | false / hold |

Research output is deliberately visible, but it cannot activate a buy/sell signal, populate the formal fair-value fields, or
become an app recommendation.

## 9. Minimum evidence required for operation

- source-verified operating tax rate or a reconciliation suitable for NOPAT;
- classified operating working-capital history and a forecast method;
- point-in-time beta methodology and traceable observation;
- documented ERP selection policy;
- pre-tax debt cost and market-value capital weights;
- complete enterprise-to-common-equity bridge;
- defensible multi-year revenue, margin, D&A, CAPEX, and working-capital paths;
- out-of-sample forecast-error calibration.

## 10. AMAT, AVGO, and QCOM expansion blockers

Before expansion, the same four-state evidence contract must be reusable without embedding GOOGL-specific field names.
AMAT needs cycle-normalized operating evidence and backlog/capex-cycle timing; AVGO needs acquisition accounting, VMware
cash-flow separation, and GAAP/non-GAAP reconciliation; QCOM needs licensing-versus-chip economics and smartphone-cycle
normalization. Each company also needs an issuer-source adapter, a complete equity bridge, independent WACC evidence, and
tests proving that missing values stay null. Expansion before those inputs exist would multiply assumption count rather than
increase verified coverage.

## 11. Twelve implementation insights and next improvements

1. **The most important output is an uncertainty map, not a midpoint.** A single midpoint compresses tax, working capital,
   reinvestment, WACC, and terminal risk into one false precision. The sensitivity surface exposes which dimensions dominate.
2. **Issuer guidance is its own epistemic class.** Guidance is stronger than an arbitrary assumption but weaker than an
   actual result. Treating it as observed data makes forecast errors invisible.
3. **A central scenario is not automatically a base fair value.** The reference path helps compare mechanics, but there is no
   empirical selection evidence that makes it more probable than the other paths.
4. **Negative early FCFF can be economically informative.** It shows the cash cost of the AI infrastructure cycle. Removing
   it through unsupported normalization would reverse the sign of the most important current investment fact.
5. **CAPEX sensitivity must separate one-year guidance from the post-guide path.** The official range constrains 2026 only;
   it says little about years two through five, which remain the larger uncertainty.
6. **Terminal-value dependence is a model-risk signal.** Most reference cells exceed the 80% threshold, so the apparent
   valuation is driven more by distant continuation assumptions than by the explicit forecast period.
7. **Data-quality uncertainty is not company risk.** Missing evidence lowers confidence and eligibility; it should not be
   hidden inside an arbitrary discount-rate premium.
8. **Growth and reinvestment require a contradiction gate.** High growth with non-positive reinvestment is rejected unless
   verified excess-capacity or asset-light evidence exists. Passing the gate does not prove the path, but failing it prevents
   internally impossible optimism.
9. **A market price cannot identify a complete operating model.** Reverse DCF is underdetermined when many variables move
   together. Reporting a unique implied growth rate without fixing the other variables would misstate identification.
10. **Enterprise value and equity value must not be mixed.** Until the bridge is complete, scenario EV cannot be divided by
    shares or compared directly to market capitalization as though they were the same claim.
11. **The next highest-value data is not another valuation formula.** A classified working-capital history, debt-cost evidence,
    and a complete equity bridge would remove more uncertainty than adding another multiple or library.
12. **Cross-company scale-up should measure verified coverage, not ticker count.** AMAT, AVGO, and QCOM should enter only
    when each field carries a source, time, state, and company-specific accounting interpretation; otherwise 200-company
    automation would merely automate unverified assumptions.

## 12. Reproduction

```powershell
python scripts/audit_googl_scenarios.py `
  --evidence docs/evidence/googl-scenarios-v2.3.json `
  --universe data/universe.csv `
  --output <isolated-research-output.json>
```

The script is offline and deterministic. It does not call SEC, a paid API, or an AI model; does not alter production data;
and does not publish a trading signal.
